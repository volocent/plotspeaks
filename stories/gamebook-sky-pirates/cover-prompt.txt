A painted storybook-adventure illustration of two wooden airships under fat patched canvas balloons, locked together by grappling ropes high above a bright sea of white clouds: one a tubby merchant ship stacked with barrels and crates, the other a sleek pirate ship with black sails and a long feathered pennant streaming in the wind. Small floating islands of grass and rock drift in the distance, one dotted with goats, and on the far horizon rises a towering wall of dark purple thundercloud with a single glint of gold behind it. Sunny blues, warm golds and creamy whites, swashbuckling and playful. Figures only tiny and seen from behind or in silhouette, no faces, no text.
