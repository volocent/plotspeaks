A painted book-cover illustration of a 1930s sleeper train crossing a high mountain viaduct at night, its long row of warm golden compartment windows curving away into falling snow, steam trailing from the locomotive. Huge moonlit peaks rise on either side, and above the pass the sky glows with faint green curtains of aurora. In the foreground, on a snowy windowsill inside one compartment, rests a single cream envelope sealed with green wax beside a small brass lamp. Deep blues and silvers with points of amber and green, elegant and mysterious, art-deco travel-poster feel. No people, no faces, no text.
