# The Star Boat

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 1):** Welcome to The Star Boat. This is a soft bedtime story. I will read it slowly and quietly, and all you need to do is listen and cuddle down in your cozy place.

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 2):** Get comfortable under your blanket; you can close your eyes. When you are ready, we will climb into the little boat, and we will count the stars together. Everything is calm and still.

## How to run this (GM instructions)

- **Voice rules (these matter most).** Read slowly and softly, in a low, even, unhurried voice. Read each beat exactly as written, one beat per paragraph, in order. Never add sound effects or music. Never add stage directions of your own.
- **Pauses.** Between beats, stay silent for one slow breath before going on; never ask a question, never wait for a reply.
- **No questions, no choices, no menus.** Do not ask anything at any point, and offer no options at the end. There is no scoring, no turn-taking and no spoken how-to-play.
- **No spoken how-to-play.** Do not offer one and do not ask; jump straight in with the cold open.
- **If the listener speaks,** answer in one soft sentence and carry on with the next beat.
- **The ending.** Stop after the last beat, with no summary. Do not say "would you like to play again" and do not offer a menu or ask for feedback. Say goodnight and stop.
- Stop phrase: "Plot Speaks, pause the story." Resume phrase: "Plot Speaks, resume." On resume, start the current beat again from its first word. (This piece has one chapter.)
- **Not for drivers.** This is for passengers, or for home. If a driver is the only listener, say gently that this is for passengers or for when the car is parked, and stop.
- **Wording.** Never say or imply that this treats, cures or helps any condition. At most it is a calm few minutes.
- Safety: this is a G piece. The listeners are children: use simple words and gentle images, and add nothing scary at all, nothing dark or lost or alone. Read only what is written.

### The script

Read the beats in order, slowly, with one slow breath of silence between them.

**Read aloud (beat 1):** Once there was a little boat made of wood, with a soft yellow sail. It sat on a quiet lake at night. The water was still and smooth, and the lake was as dark as a blueberry, and as calm as a sleeping cat.

**Read aloud (beat 2):** You climb in, and the boat gives a tiny wobble, and then it is still. There is a cozy blanket on the seat; you pull it up to your chin. It is warm and soft, and it smells like home.

**Read aloud (beat 3):** The little boat begins to float; it lifts up, up, up off the water, slow as a feather. The lake grows small beneath you. Soon the boat is floating in the big, quiet sky.

**Read aloud (beat 4):** The sky is deep blue, like a velvet blanket. It is not cold, and it is not windy; the air is gentle. The boat rocks softly, side to side, the way a cradle does.

**Read aloud (beat 5):** The boat has a little lantern, hanging from the mast, and it glows the color of honey. It lights your blanket and your hands, and it does not shine too bright. It is just enough for a kind, quiet night.

**Read aloud (beat 6):** A star twinkles close by; it is a small, round, golden star, as friendly as a nightlight. The boat drifts up beside it; you whisper, one. The star glows a little brighter, to say hello.

**Read aloud (beat 7):** The boat drifts on; here is another star, a silvery one, shining like a little button. You whisper, two; the star blinks slowly, as if it is saying goodnight. Two stars, and you are cozy.

**Read aloud (beat 8):** Next, three stars sit close together, like three friends sharing a secret. They twinkle all at once; you whisper, three. The boat floats gently past them, and they wave their light.

**Read aloud (beat 9):** A soft cloud drifts by, white and fluffy; it brushes the boat very lightly and moves on. Behind it, a warm pink star peeks out; you whisper, four. It glows like the inside of a seashell.

**Read aloud (beat 10):** The boat sails on, slow and sleepy. Here comes a tiny blue star, shy and small; you whisper, five. The blue star hums a tiny hum, so quiet you can hardly hear it, like a lullaby.

**Read aloud (beat 11):** A little night bird glides by on silent wings, and gives you a sleepy nod. It is going home to its nest, and it is not in any hurry. Neither are you, in your boat with its soft yellow sail.

**Read aloud (beat 12):** The moon is a soft curve in the sky now, smiling down. It makes a path of pale light, and the boat floats along it. The path is smooth and glittery, like moonlit milk.

**Read aloud (beat 13):** Along the path, two more stars appear. A green one, and a yellow one; you whisper, six, and seven. They twinkle together, taking turns, one and then the other, like gentle breathing.

**Read aloud (beat 14):** The boat is very sleepy now, and so are you; your eyes feel heavy, and the blanket is warm. Another star shines ahead, a big soft one, round and pearly. You whisper, eight.

**Read aloud (beat 15):** Then comes a star that is lavender, the color of bedtime. It glows slowly, in and out; you whisper, nine. The boat rocks, and rocks, and the sky is quiet and kind.

**Read aloud (beat 16):** Now the boat floats slowly through a soft cloud, as cozy as the inside of a pillow. It is white and warm and quiet. You feel your arms grow heavy, and your toes grow warm, and your breath grow slow.

**Read aloud (beat 17):** The last star is the biggest and the gentlest. It shines like a lamp in a window; you whisper, ten. It tells you, in a light that needs no words, that you are safe and loved.

**Read aloud (beat 18):** The little boat begins to float back down, slow as a feather, slow as a sigh. The lake waits below, smooth and dark. The boat touches the water with hardly a ripple.

**Read aloud (beat 19):** The stars shine on above you, ten friendly lights, keeping watch while you rest. The boat sways, and the blanket is warm; everything is quiet; thank you for listening. Goodnight.
