# The Sleepy Garden

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 1):** Welcome to The Sleepy Garden. This is a soft bedtime story. I will read it slowly and quietly, and all you need to do is listen and snuggle down in your cozy place.

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 2):** Get comfortable under your blanket; you can close your eyes. When you are ready, we will tiptoe into the garden, and we will say goodnight to everyone in it. Everything is calm and still.

### The script

Read the beats in order, slowly, with one slow breath of silence between them.

**Read aloud (beat 1):** There is a little garden behind a little house. The sun is going down, and the sky is pink and gold; the garden is very quiet and calm. It is almost time for everyone in it to go to sleep.

**Read aloud (beat 2):** You tiptoe through the little wooden gate; the grass is soft under your feet, and the air smells sweet. A tiny breeze says shh, shh, through the leaves. It is the gentlest breeze there is.

**Read aloud (beat 3):** First you come to the daisies. All day they have held their white faces up to the sun; now they are tired. One by one, they fold their petals in, close, and tuck their heads down.

**Read aloud (beat 4):** The daisies whisper, goodnight; you whisper, goodnight, daisies. They are fast asleep in a moment, all in a row, like little white cups turned over for the night. Everything is calm and still.

**Read aloud (beat 5):** Next to them stands a tall sunflower, as tall as a door. Its big golden head leans slowly down; it is heavy with sleep. It nods once, twice, and says, goodnight, little one.

**Read aloud (beat 6):** In the clover lives a small gray bunny; its ears are long and soft. It yawns a big yawn, and its whiskers twitch. It wriggles down into the green and curls up round and warm.

**Read aloud (beat 7):** By the garden path, a row of little purple flowers is nodding. They are the sleepiest flowers of all. They bow their heads very low and say, goodnight, goodnight, in a soft and gentle sing-song.

**Read aloud (beat 8):** The bunny says, goodnight, in a tiny whisper; you whisper, goodnight, bunny. It closes its eyes, and its nose goes still; its nose has stopped twitching. The bunny is fast asleep.

**Read aloud (beat 9):** Over on a mossy stone sits a round hedgehog. It is as round as a ball of wool; it tucks in its nose, then its paws. Then it rolls up tight and snug, and it gives a soft snuffle.

**Read aloud (beat 10):** The hedgehog says, goodnight, in a sleepy snuffle; you whisper, goodnight, hedgehog; then it is quiet. A small round ball, warm in the moss, breathing slowly in and out. Everything is calm and still.

**Read aloud (beat 11):** On a big green leaf, a snail is slowly, slowly going home. It carries its little house on its back. It reaches the edge of the leaf, tucks in, and pulls its door closed.

**Read aloud (beat 12):** The snail says, goodnight, in the smallest voice you ever heard; you whisper, goodnight, snail. Inside its shell it is dark, and warm, and just right for sleeping. Everything is calm and still.

**Read aloud (beat 13):** Under the hedge, a family of sleepy mice peeks out. They have been busy all day. Now they yawn, one after another, a small yawn, and a smaller one, and the smallest of all, and they curl up together.

**Read aloud (beat 14):** Up in the old apple tree, a little brown bird sits on a branch. It puffs up its feathers, until it looks like a soft round pillow. It tucks its head under its wing.

**Read aloud (beat 15):** The bird sings one last sleepy note, and then it is still. You whisper, goodnight, little bird. The branch rocks it gently in the evening breeze, back and forth, back and forth.

**Read aloud (beat 16):** The fireflies come out now, tiny and golden. They glow on and off, slowly, like little lanterns drifting by; they do not make a sound. They only light the way, as the garden falls asleep.

**Read aloud (beat 17):** The moon rises over the fence, round and kind. Its pale light spills across the flowers and the grass. Everything in the garden is resting now, safe and warm, tucked into the dark.

**Read aloud (beat 18):** The ladybug is the last one awake. It walks along a blade of grass, slow and careful, until it finds a leaf shaped like a little bed. It folds its wings, and says, goodnight, in a speck of a voice.

**Read aloud (beat 19):** You tiptoe back to the little gate, and close it softly. The whole garden sleeps behind you; now it is your turn; snuggle down, and breathe slowly; thank you for listening. Goodnight.
