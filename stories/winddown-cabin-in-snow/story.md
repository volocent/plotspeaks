# Cabin in the Snow

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 1):** Welcome to Cabin in the Snow. This is a slow, cozy story for the end of the day. I will read softly, and you only need to listen and picture it as we go along.

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 2):** Get comfortable, and let your eyes close if you like. We will step inside the cabin whenever you are ready, and we will take our time, with nothing to hurry for.

## How to run this (GM instructions)

- **Voice rules (these matter most).** Read slowly and softly, in a low, even, unhurried voice. Read each beat exactly as written, one beat per paragraph, in order. Never add sound effects or music. Never add stage directions of your own.
- **Pauses.** Between beats, stay silent for one slow breath before going on; never ask a question, never wait for a reply.
- **No questions, no choices, no menus.** Do not ask anything at any point, and offer no options at the end. There is no scoring, no turn-taking and no spoken how-to-play.
- **No spoken how-to-play.** Do not offer one and do not ask; jump straight in with the cold open.
- **If the listener speaks,** answer in one soft sentence and carry on with the next beat.
- **The ending.** Stop after the last beat, with no summary. Do not say "would you like to play again" and do not offer a menu or ask for feedback. Say goodnight and stop.
- Stop phrase: "Plot Speaks, pause the story." Resume phrase: "Plot Speaks, resume." On resume, start the current beat again from its first word. (This piece has one chapter.)
- **Not for drivers.** This is for passengers, or for home. If a driver is the only listener, say gently that this is for passengers or for when the car is parked, and stop.
- **Wording.** Never say or imply that this treats, cures or helps any condition. At most it is a calm few minutes.
- Safety: this is a G piece. Add nothing frightening, and no sudden or loud moments. Read only what is written.

### The script

Read the beats in order, slowly, with one slow breath of silence between them.

**Read aloud (beat 1):** Imagine a small wooden cabin at the edge of a quiet forest. It is evening, and snow is falling slowly, large soft flakes drifting down through the dark blue air. The whole world is hushed.

**Read aloud (beat 2):** You are standing at the cabin door; the wood is warm to the touch, though the night is cold. Behind you, the snow is thick and untouched. You take a long breath, and it makes a small cloud.

**Read aloud (beat 3):** You open the door and step inside; the warmth wraps around you at once, like a hug. The air smells of woodsmoke and pine and something sweet. You stamp the snow from your boots, and close the door.

**Read aloud (beat 4):** The cabin has one room, small and snug. The walls are made of golden logs, worn smooth; a lamp glows low on a table. Everything here is simple, and everything is exactly where it should be.

**Read aloud (beat 5):** In the corner is a stone fireplace, and in it a fire burns steady and low. The flames curl and lean, orange and gold. Now and then a log settles with a soft crackle, and sparks drift upward.

**Read aloud (beat 6):** You take off your coat and hang it on a wooden peg. Your boots go by the door, side by side. You feel your shoulders drop, as the last of the day's cold slips away from you.

**Read aloud (beat 7):** You walk across the floor in your socks. The boards are smooth and a little creaky, and they are warm near the fire. Each step is slow and soft, and you feel your whole body begin to unwind.

**Read aloud (beat 8):** Before the fire is a deep, old armchair, and over its arm lies a thick wool blanket. It is the color of oatmeal, with a soft stripe of blue. You lift it, and it is warm already.

**Read aloud (beat 9):** You sink into the chair, and it holds you completely. You draw the blanket up over your knees, then your waist, then your shoulders. It is heavy in the gentlest way, like being tucked in.

**Read aloud (beat 10):** The fire glows on your face. You can feel the heat on your hands and on your feet, soft and steady. Your toes curl a little in your thick socks, and then they stretch out and rest.

**Read aloud (beat 11):** On the small table beside you sits a mug. It holds something warm and sweet, and a little steam rises from it; you wrap your hands around it. The warmth spreads through your palms and up your arms.

**Read aloud (beat 12):** On the shelf above the fire sit a few old books and a little wooden bird. A pair of woolen mittens dries on a hook, steaming softly. It is a home that has been kept kind for a very long time.

**Read aloud (beat 13):** Outside the window, the snow keeps falling. You watch the flakes pass through the glow of the lamp, slow and silver, one after another. They land on the sill and rest there, softly piling up.

**Read aloud (beat 14):** The wind is hardly there at all, only a faint hush against the roof. The whole cabin seems to be listening to it. Inside, nothing stirs but the quiet dance of the fire.

**Read aloud (beat 15):** A clock on the wall ticks, slowly and evenly; tick, and tick, and tick. It is a friendly sound, the sound of time passing without any hurry. You let your breath settle into its slow rhythm.

**Read aloud (beat 16):** The fire shifts, and a small shower of sparks floats up the chimney. You watch them go, bright for a moment, and then gone. Your thoughts are like that too, drifting up and away, without any effort.

**Read aloud (beat 17):** Your eyes follow the flames for a while. Then they drift to the rafters above, where the shadows move gently; the room is dim and gold. Your eyelids feel heavy, and you let them be heavy.

**Read aloud (beat 18):** The mug is empty now, and you set it down. The blanket is warm around your chin; the chair is deep and kind. Your body feels loose and calm, from your shoulders down to your toes.

**Read aloud (beat 19):** Beyond the walls, the forest is wrapped in white; the pines wear their snow like sleeves. Every track has been covered over, and every sound has been softened. The night is wide and peaceful.

**Read aloud (beat 20):** The fire sinks a little lower, glowing red and gold. You do not need to add a log, or check the door. Everything is as it should be, and the cabin is safe and warm around you.

**Read aloud (beat 21):** A soft quilt is folded at the foot of a small bed in the loft, and a pillow waits there, plump and cool. You know you could climb up whenever you like. But for now, you simply enjoy the chair.

**Read aloud (beat 22):** You stay here in the quiet, in the soft light, under the heavy blanket, while the snow falls on and on. The day is done, and you can let it be done; thank you for listening. Goodnight.
