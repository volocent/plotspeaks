A vast stair cut down into the face of a sheer grey mountain cliff, landing after landing descending into shadow, with a monastery-city of stone terraces and small lit windows clinging to the rock above it; a sea of white cloud fills the valley below in golden morning light, white frost creeps up the lower steps in midsummer, and far down the stair a faint silver glow shines from a sealed round stone door; tiny lantern lights of a small party starting down the steps, seen from far away, wondrous and adventurous mood.
