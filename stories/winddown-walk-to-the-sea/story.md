# A Walk to the Sea

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 1):** Welcome to A Walk to the Sea. This is a slow, quiet story for the end of the day. I will read softly, and you only need to listen and picture it as we go along.

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 2):** Settle in however feels comfortable. Let your eyes close if you like. We will begin the walk whenever you are ready, and we will take it slowly, one step at a time.

## How to run this (GM instructions)

- **Voice rules (these matter most).** Read slowly and softly, in a low, even, unhurried voice. Read each beat exactly as written, one beat per paragraph, in order. Never add sound effects or music. Never add stage directions of your own.
- **Pauses.** Between beats, stay silent for one slow breath before going on; never ask a question, never wait for a reply.
- **No questions, no choices, no menus.** Do not ask anything at any point, and offer no options at the end. There is no scoring, no turn-taking and no spoken how-to-play.
- **No spoken how-to-play.** Do not offer one and do not ask; jump straight in with the cold open.
- **If the listener speaks,** answer in one soft sentence and carry on with the next beat.
- **The ending.** Stop after the last beat, with no summary. Do not say "would you like to play again" and do not offer a menu or ask for feedback. Say goodnight and stop.
- Stop phrase: "Plot Speaks, pause the story." Resume phrase: "Plot Speaks, resume." On resume, start the current beat again from its first word. (This piece has one chapter.)
- **Not for drivers.** This is for passengers, or for home. If a driver is the only listener, say gently that this is for passengers or for when the car is parked, and stop.
- **Wording.** Never say or imply that this treats, cures or helps any condition. At most it is a calm few minutes.
- Safety: this is a G piece. Add nothing frightening, and no sudden or loud moments. Read only what is written.

### The script

Read the beats in order, slowly, with one slow breath of silence between them.

**Read aloud (beat 1):** Imagine you are standing at the top of a small path, just as the day is ending. The air is mild and soft; the sky is the color of pale peaches. You have nowhere to be but here.

**Read aloud (beat 2):** You begin to walk; the path is made of packed sand, smooth and warm underfoot. Each step is slow and easy. You let your arms swing loosely, and you let your breath come and go by itself.

**Read aloud (beat 3):** On either side of the path, tall grass sways gently in the evening breeze. The blades are silvery, and they brush quietly together. Somewhere far off, a bird calls once and then grows still.

**Read aloud (beat 4):** A little way along, you pass a bush of pale wild roses. Their petals are closing for the night; the scent is faint and sweet. You breathe it in, and it seems to slow your steps even more.

**Read aloud (beat 5):** The path bends gently to the left, and you follow it without thinking. The sand grows a little cooler; the light is softer now, golden at the edges. You walk on, step after step, unhurried.

**Read aloud (beat 6):** A wooden fence runs beside you for a while, its posts gray and smooth from years of weather. You rest your fingers on the top rail as you pass. The wood is still warm from the day.

**Read aloud (beat 7):** The ground slopes gently downward. You can smell something new in the air, a clean, salty freshness. It is the sea, still out of sight, and the thought of it makes your shoulders loosen.

**Read aloud (beat 8):** The sound of your footsteps is soft and slow, a gentle crunch and a hush. You notice that your breath has matched your steps. In for a few steps, and out for a few more, easy as walking.

**Read aloud (beat 9):** Now you hear it, very faintly; a slow whisper, rising and falling. It is the sound of small waves, a long way off. You keep walking, and the sound grows slowly closer, soft and steady.

**Read aloud (beat 10):** The path winds between two low dunes; the sand here is pale and fine. A little lantern hangs on a post ahead, glowing a warm yellow. It lights the way, though the sky is still bright.

**Read aloud (beat 11):** You step past the lantern, and the dunes open out. Before you is a wide, quiet beach, and beyond it the sea, calm and silvery; no one else is here. The whole shore is yours.

**Read aloud (beat 12):** You slip off your shoes and let your feet sink into the sand. It is cool on top and still faintly warm underneath. You wiggle your toes, and the sand shifts around them, soft as flour.

**Read aloud (beat 13):** You walk slowly toward the water; the sand grows damp and firm. Small shells lie here and there, pale pink and cream; you do not need to pick one up. You just notice them, and walk on.

**Read aloud (beat 14):** A little further along, a smooth gray stone lies half buried in the sand. It is warm from the sun. You pick it up and hold it, feeling its weight in your palm, and then you set it down again.

**Read aloud (beat 15):** At the edge of the sea, a thin wave slides up the sand, and then slips back again. It leaves a lace of foam that melts away. You stand and watch it come and go, again and again.

**Read aloud (beat 16):** You find a smooth, flat place a little way back from the water, and you sit down. The sand holds you gently. You pull your knees up, or stretch your legs out, whichever feels better.

**Read aloud (beat 17):** The sky is changing; the peach has turned to rose, and the rose to lavender. Out over the sea, the first star appears, small and clear. You watch it, and your breathing grows slow.

**Read aloud (beat 18):** The waves keep their rhythm; in, and out; in, and out. Each one is a little like a breath. You find your own breath slowing to match it, long and quiet and easy.

**Read aloud (beat 19):** A small crab, the color of sand, scuttles out of a tiny hole, and then back in again. You smile, and you stay very still. The beach is full of quiet, busy lives, all settling in for the night.

**Read aloud (beat 20):** A soft breeze passes over you, carrying the smell of salt. It lifts your hair for a moment and sets it down again. You feel calm, and safe, and pleasantly tired, as the light goes.

**Read aloud (beat 21):** More stars come out, one by one, until the sky is scattered with them. The sea turns the deep blue of evening. The lantern behind you glows warm on the dune, a small friendly light.

**Read aloud (beat 22):** You lie back on the sand, and look up at the wide sky. The stars are many now; the sea hums its low song. Your arms and legs are heavy, and you feel held by the whole quiet shore.

**Read aloud (beat 23):** You sit a while longer, listening to the water. There is nothing to do, and nothing to decide. The day has finished its work, and the sea carries it gently away.

**Read aloud (beat 24):** When you are ready, the path will be there behind you, lit by the lantern, waiting. For now, you can stay here, with the waves and the stars; thank you for listening. Goodnight.
