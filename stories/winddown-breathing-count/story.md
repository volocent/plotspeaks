# Count of Ten

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 1):** Welcome to Count of Ten. This is a few calm minutes of slow breathing, for the end of the day. I will read softly, and you only need to listen and breathe along with me.

**Read aloud (cold open, beat 2):** Sit or lie down in a way that feels easy. Let your shoulders drop, and let your hands rest. We will start whenever you are ready, and there is nothing to get right.

## How to run this (GM instructions)

- **Voice rules (these matter most).** Read slowly and softly, in a low, even, unhurried voice. Read each beat exactly as written, one beat per paragraph, in order. Never add sound effects or music. Never add stage directions of your own.
- **Pauses.** Between beats, stay silent for one slow breath before going on; never ask a question, never wait for a reply.
- **No questions, no choices, no menus.** Do not ask anything at any point, and offer no options at the end. There is no scoring, no turn-taking and no spoken how-to-play.
- **No spoken how-to-play.** Do not offer one and do not ask; jump straight in with the cold open.
- **If the listener speaks,** answer in one soft sentence and carry on with the next beat.
- **The ending.** Stop after the last beat, with no summary. Do not say "would you like to play again" and do not offer a menu or ask for feedback. Say goodnight and stop.
- Stop phrase: "Plot Speaks, pause the story." Resume phrase: "Plot Speaks, resume." On resume, start the current beat again from its first word. (This piece has one chapter.)
- **Not for drivers.** This is for passengers, or for home. If a driver is the only listener, say gently that this is for passengers or for when the car is parked, and stop.
- **Wording.** Never say or imply that this treats, cures or helps any condition. At most it is a calm few minutes.
- Safety: this is a G piece. Add nothing frightening, and no sudden or loud moments. Read only what is written.

### The script

Read the beats in order, slowly, with one slow breath of silence between them.

**Read aloud (beat 1):** Let your breathing be natural for a moment; there is no right way to do this; let your hands rest. Let your eyes close, or let them look softly at one place. Nothing is expected of you.

**Read aloud (beat 2):** We will count our breaths together, from one to ten. Each breath has an in and an out; the count comes on the out breath; if you lose your place, that is fine. You simply start again at one.

**Read aloud (beat 3):** Breathe in slowly through your nose, and out gently. That is one; breathe in, and let the air go; that is two. Let each breath be a little slower than the one before it, and a little softer.

**Read aloud (beat 4):** Breathe in, and out; that is three; breathe in, and out; that is four. Notice the cool air as it comes in and the warmer air as it leaves. Let your shoulders keep softening, with each slow breath.

**Read aloud (beat 5):** In, and out; that is five; in, and out; that is six. Halfway through, there is no hurry. Let the out breath be long and quiet, like a sigh you do not have to make a sound for.

**Read aloud (beat 6):** Notice the little pause at the end of each out breath, before the next one begins. It is a still, quiet moment; you do not need to make it longer or shorter. Just let it be there.

**Read aloud (beat 7):** In, and out; that is seven; in, and out; that is eight. If your mind wanders, that is what minds do. Simply come back to the breath, as gently as you would pick up a dropped feather.

**Read aloud (beat 8):** In, and out; that is nine; in, and out; that is ten. Now let us begin again at one; breathe in slowly, and out gently; that is one; breathe in, and out; that is two. Easy and slow.

**Read aloud (beat 9):** In, and out; that is three; in, and out; that is four. Let your belly rise a little on the way in, and fall on the way out; let your jaw be loose. Let your hands be heavy.

**Read aloud (beat 10):** In, and out; that is five; in, and out; that is six. Each breath is its own small wave, rising and falling; you do not have to hold onto any of them. Let each one go.

**Read aloud (beat 11):** In, and out; that is one again. Let your breath be small and quiet now; in, and out; that is two. You are doing nothing at all except breathing, and that is exactly enough.

**Read aloud (beat 12):** In, and out; that is three; in, and out; that is four. In, and out; that is five. Let your thoughts drift past like leaves on a slow river, and keep your attention on the soft rise and fall.

**Read aloud (beat 13):** In, and out; that is seven; in, and out; that is eight; in, and out; that is nine; in, and out; that is ten. Let your breath return to its own pace now; thank you for listening. Goodnight.
