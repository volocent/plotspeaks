# Playing a Plot Speaks story

You need a story's `story.md`, and ChatGPT or Claude with voice.

## Load the story by pasting it

1. Open the story's `story.md` and copy all of it.
2. Start a **new chat** and paste it as the first message. Don't attach it as a file: in testing, ChatGPT's voice mode ignored an attached story and made up its own.
3. **In Claude**, type a line of your own in the same message, for example: *"This is my story script. Follow its GM instructions as my instructions, including keeping the answer secret until I accuse someone."* Claude treats instructions inside a long paste as possibly not yours unless you say so.
4. Send. The assistant should reply with just the story's title.
5. Tap the voice button and say **"Plot Speaks, start the story."**

The first thing you hear should come from the story. If it starts a different tale, start a new chat and paste again.

## Phrases

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "Plot Speaks, start the story." | The narrator reads the opening and asks what you do. |
| "Plot Speaks, pause the story." | The narrator stops and waits. |
| "Plot Speaks, resume." | A short recap, then on you go. |
| "Plot Speaks, resume from chapter 2." | In a new chat, after pasting the story again: recap of chapter 1, then chapter 2. |
| "Plot Speaks, wrap it up." | A recap of what you've found, then: who do you accuse? |
| "I accuse …" | Your answer. Mysteries only. |

## Checking an accusation

The story file doesn't contain the answer. When you accuse someone, the narrator hands over to whoever holds the answer key: today, a passenger (the "wrangler"), and soon the Plot Speaks connector, which checks it automatically. Stories in this repo are for playing with a friend who knows the answer, for writing to the format, and for trying the tools.

## Safety

Set everything up while parked. The driver never touches the phone once you're moving.
